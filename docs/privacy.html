RETURN Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

RETURN is designed to be local-first. The current version does not operate an account system, analytics service, advertising network, or cloud database.

Information stored on your device

Your Why Vault responses, reflection entries, taper-plan settings, and notification preference are stored locally on your device.

Screen Time selections

RETURN uses Apple's Family Controls, Device Activity, and Managed Settings frameworks. When you select apps, categories, or websites, Apple provides RETURN with privacy-preserving opaque tokens rather than readable app identities. RETURN stores those tokens in its private App Group so its Screen Time extension can enforce the limit you chose.

Audio and notifications

Guided audio is generated on-device with Apple's speech system. Evening reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device.

Data collection and tracking

The current version of RETURN does not send your Why Vault, reflections, Screen Time selection tokens, or usage-monitoring configuration to a developer-operated server. RETURN does not use third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.

Deleting your data

You can use Reset RETURN in Settings to remove the app's local program data, Screen Time selection, monitoring configuration, and active RETURN shields. Uninstalling the app also removes its app-local data according to iOS behavior.

Apple services

Apple's operating system frameworks process Screen Time authorization and activity data under Apple's own terms and privacy policies.

Changes

If a future version adds cloud sync, analytics, accounts, or other data processing, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures must be updated before release.
