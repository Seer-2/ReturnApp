## Description
RETURN helps you spend less time on distracting apps without demanding an all-or-nothing detox.

Start by choosing the apps and categories you want to protect, estimating your current daily use, and setting a realistic target. RETURN builds a gradual weekly taper and uses Apple's Screen Time frameworks to enforce the daily allowance you choose.

RETURN is designed around deliberate change rather than streaks or punishment:

• Gradual weekly limits that move from your baseline toward your goal
• Private app and category selection through Apple's Family Controls picker
• Automatic Screen Time shielding after your daily allowance is reached
• A Why Vault that keeps your own reasons for changing visible
• Daily reflection prompts to notice triggers and patterns
• Short audio resets generated on-device
• Local notifications for an evening check-in
• Local-first storage for your written reflections and Why Vault

Privacy is part of the design. RETURN does not receive readable names for the apps you select through Screen Time; Apple provides opaque tokens. Your Why Vault and reflections remain on your device in this version.

RETURN is a productivity and habit-awareness tool. It does not diagnose, treat, or provide medical care for addiction or any other condition.
